“Farmers Helping Farmers” Conference + Field Day

A two-day broadacre sponsorship with an existing partnership behind it — committed and paid since December/January. Day one is a conference room with our stand in it; day two is a field day on the Lawless property at Burramine with no stands at all. The money is spent, so the work left is registration, campaign prep and getting the right assets to Mulwala.

The play

VicNoTill puts progressive broadacre growers, soil scientists and agronomists in one room for two days, and the room is already sympathetic to how we talk about soil. That makes it a conversation event rather than a promotion event. Bruce and James work the stand and the sessions on day one; day two is paddock time and networking with no stand to hold anyone in place.

Lunch is catered among the sponsor stands on day one, which is our foot-traffic window — the same lesson the Cherry and FGV rollouts kept surfacing. Plan the stand and the team’s roaming around the breaks.

Goals: build brand awareness · network with progressive broadacre growers · generate qualified leads · support the no-till community we already sit inside.

Offer tiers

No giveaway this time. The activation is a two-tier offer, the same shape that ran at Fruit Growers Victoria — one open to every stand visitor, one held back for the conversations worth holding it back for. The landing pages still need a decision from the team.

Everyone · Nutrient Audit draw
Every booth visitor goes into the draw to win a Nutrient Audit. We pick the winner ourselves, so the best opportunities can be cherry-picked rather than left to chance. Gives every grower a reason to hand over details and positions the team as advisors.
Trigger → any grower who visits the stand and enters the draw.
Reserved · we match your 2026 testing investment
Dollar figure against the top three opportunities. Not advertised — kept up the sleeve of the program manager and offered only to growers who show genuine potential. Landing page: hybridag.com.au/testing-investment-match
Trigger → highest-potential conversation, program manager’s call.
Execution: two separate QR codes routing to two separate landing pages, one per tier, and two A4 QR posters printed for the stand. Keep the testing-investment offer off to the side. Open: confirm the offer and landing pages with the team before the posters go to print.
