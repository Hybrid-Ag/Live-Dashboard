Three days as an exhibitor on a 20m × 20m outdoor site, shared three ways with SA Liquid Systems and CustomVac. Application, certificate of currency, site and exhibitor fee are all done. What is left is the campaign, the stand build and the logistics.

The play

2025 was a 10m × 10m site put together at short notice. The 2025 SWOT said the single biggest fix was to book early and get a better position, and that is what this year does: a 20m × 20m site locked in and paid, with two partners sharing it. The other lesson that came through was how well growing things and moving things pull people in, and the site is built around exactly that — our own grow display, with the SA Liquid Systems liquid inject rig running alongside it.

Goals: build brand awareness · network with broadacre growers · generate qualified leads.

Nathan · Colouring-in competitionKeeping the kids busy keeps the conversation flowing. Add a prize and it’s a crowd pleaser.
Jamie · Anything MilwaukeeHuge crowd pleaser. A tent or flag out the front would draw people; an actual product as a giveaway always goes down well.
Nathan · Liquid product displayAt Hi-Tech Ag, small plastic bottles filled not quite to the top, with particular labels, ranged across the table. Growers picked them up and shook them — a great talking point.
