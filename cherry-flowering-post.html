Draft caption

How evenly is your cherry block flowering?

Two cherry blocks can be at different stages on the same day. As bloom progresses, check flowering uniformity within each cultivar, the overlap with compatible pollinisers and the conditions for bee activity. Keep those observations with the block’s weather, soil moisture and nutrition records.

If Differential Sap Analysis (DSA) is part of your program, keep the planned flowering checks. Read the results alongside growth stage and recent inputs before deciding what to change.

Talk to your Hybrid Ag agronomist about the stage your blocks have reached and the next check in your program.

Source notes

  • Australian Cherry Production Guide. Pollination and bee activity, pp. 73–75; growth and development, p. 76. Supports reading cultivar and block conditions alongside flowering stage.
  • NovaCropControl sweet cherry sampling guide, pp. 2–3. Supports paired sampling of the youngest fully developed and oldest still-vital leaves. Use the current Hybrid Ag sampling kit instructions for collection, packing and dispatch.
  • Hybrid Ag Bud & Flower review package, July 2026. Paired results need crop-stage, conditions and program context. Its DSA graph is illustrative, not a universal sampling schedule.
  • Hybrid Ag Cherry Nutrition Playbook, p. 42 (internal source). The final Cherry Nutrition Playbook, p. 42, includes DSA checks at 20–30% flowering, 80–90% flowering and shuck fall. This concept retains flowering checks where they are part of the agreed program.
