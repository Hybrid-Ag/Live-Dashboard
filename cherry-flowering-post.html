Draft caption

The new leaves need sugar too.

During cherry flowering, developing leaves draw on stored carbohydrates alongside flowers and young fruit. As the canopy develops, photosynthesis contributes more of the sugars needed for growth.

That makes leaf development part of the flowering assessment. When bloom is moving ahead but the canopy is slow to develop, the useful question is what is limiting it. A nutrient shortage, poor root conditions and damaged leaves can require different responses.

Your Hybrid Ag agronomist can help interpret those observations alongside the block’s nutrition records and developing crop load, then decide whether the program needs to change.

Calendar timing
September–October is the regional planning window. Follow bloom and leaf development in each block; timing varies with site, cultivar and season.
