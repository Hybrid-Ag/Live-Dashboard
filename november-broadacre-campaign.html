Soil testing checklist

Use Jimmy’s field video and this checklist to bring in soil-testing enquiries and give growers with an existing report a reason to call. The worked example shows how we get from a shortfall in kg/ha to a decision about what to put out this season.

Download draft checklist · PDFRead the three pages ↗

What the grower gets

  1. Choose your test set and targets wiselyCheck the panel covers what you want to find out. Use yield records and DataFarming imagery to pick a strong patch and a weak one, then walk them. Sample contrasting zones and depths separately.
  2. Turn the readings into Nutrient requirements in kg/haHybrid-Ag calculates the kg/ha shortfall for each relevant Nutrient and explains the soil target behind it. The report shows the total correction. The crop and the budget help determine how much to tackle this season.
  3. Adjust the program and choose the placementCount what’s already going out first. If there’s still a gap, compare what a Hybrid-Ag prescription liquid or dry blend, including granules, would deliver at a practical rate. Check placement with the gear on hand and cost per hectare.

Draft ad copy

What would your soil test change?

If the same patch falls behind each year, start there. Our Broadacre soil testing checklist covers where to sample, how we calculate what you’re short in kg/ha and what that might change in the paddock this season.

Download the checklist for the example soil report. If you’d like to talk through results you already have or plan new sampling, tick the call option. You can also ask for details of the soil-testing webinar.

The worked report and the two approaches

Page two gives growers the test panel and four questions they can take to Jimmy or Bruce. Page three works through one 0–10 cm sample. The figures describe the total soil correction for that sample. Before deciding on a rate for this season, check what the planned inputs already cover.

In the blend example, Calcium and Sulphur are both short. If the crop still needs both and liquid placement suits the grower’s gear, Liqui-Gyp is one option to assess. Check what the proposed blend would actually deliver per hectare.

Sampling, targeting and follow-up

Jimmy’s starting point is a recurring production difference. Use DataFarming NDVI imagery, paddock history and yield records to choose areas to walk. GPS-mark the sampling sites. If roots stop at a layer, agree depths with the lab and keep the samples separate. A field pH check can help locate a layer worth testing.

Jimmy / Soil testing — draft script

If the same patch keeps falling behind, that’s where I’d start. Pick a patch that usually yields well too. We use DataFarming imagery and yield records to pick the spots, then walk them before we sample. Keep the samples separate so you can see what’s different.

When the results come back, at Hybrid-Ag we calculate the Nutrient shortfall in kilos per hectare for you. That’s the total correction for the soil we sampled. What’s worth putting out this season depends on the crop, what you’ve already got going out and where your gear can put it.

We’ve put the soil panel and a worked example in a short checklist. Grab a copy below. If you’ve already got a report, have it handy and ask for a chat with our Broadacre team. You can tick the webinar box too if you’d like the details once they’re set.
