The crop before harvest

  1. 01

    What can I still influence between now and harvest?

    Start with the observed stage and the development still ahead of that variety. The stage examples are illustrative, not claims about current regional conditions.

  2. 02

    What might be preventing the fruit quality I want?

    Consider the actual concern alongside crop load, canopy condition and water supply. A Nutrient result alone does not establish the cause of a fruit-quality problem.

Calcium and DSA

  1. 03

    Is my Calcium management supporting fruit development?

    Explain Calcium movement and its limits. A leaf-sap Calcium result does not establish Calcium delivery into the fruit or predict firmness.

  2. 04

    What can DSA tell me about the tree’s Nutrient status?

    Compare the separately collected leaf-age samples using the agreed protocol. Use the historical Calcium trend to discuss change over time, then interpret it alongside actual stage and management history. A difference is not automatically a deficiency.

From evidence to a decision

  1. 05

    What practical decisions can I make from that information?

    Work through the three-date Royal Tioga paired-leaf record, revealing the suggested checks and prompts as the discussion develops. Use the source-recorded stages to consider what else needs checking. A supported Nutrient need may justify a standard Hybrid-Ag product or custom blend; more information or no nutrition change may be appropriate.

These questions and the technical answers remain for Andrew’s review. The deck does not assume harvesting is underway.
