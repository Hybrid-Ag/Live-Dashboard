Three decisions

Everything we could do with these leads comes down to three choices. Our recommendation is marked on each; the case for and against sits underneath. Make the three calls and the route below fills itself in.

Decision 1 · the offer

Free test, or paid test credited?

A · Free spring DSA Recommended

One paired young-leaf / old-leaf DSA on a block of their choice, on us, read back by Josh, Chelsea or Nate. Capped at the first 15 growers or managers.

For Removes the last objection; the read-back is where the program conversation starts; one test costs a fraction of the first order it opens. Against Real lab and freight cost, so the cap matters; spring is the agronomists’ busiest time to interpret.

B · Grower pays, credited on the first order

Pia’s idea and the Henty Testing Investment Match are the same lever: they pay for one block test, the full amount comes off their first order that season.

For Self-qualifying, only growers who mean it pay; the credit is tied to an order; the Henty landing page already exists. Against Asks a $2 lead for money before we have spoken; take-up will be low; six conditions to administer; competes with the free audit the ads already promise.

Decision 2 · the reach

Worked, or offered?

A · Josh phones every grower and manager Recommended

Josh books 15-minute block calls with the 24 growers and managers, Chelsea where a block calls for her, and phones the ones who don’t book. He is new, agronomy-trained and in the office; this is the best use of that time.

For No hard cost; a call is a qualification and a sales conversation in one; Josh learns the apple accounts from day one. Against He needs a script and Chelsea behind him on technical questions; a 15-minute call needs a next step (Decision 1) behind it.

B · Offered by email only

The offer goes out in the Playbook email and we wait for replies. Same rungs, slower, and most of the 37 stay Playbook-only.

For Zero time cost; nothing to coordinate. Against Cherry’s 62 Playbook-only leads got one email and went nowhere; we already know how this ends.

Add-ons for people who won’t take a call, no decision needed today: a one-hour Andrew Smith spring Q&A online, and a free half-hour block walk when Chelsea or Nate are next through Goulburn/Yarra or the Adelaide Hills (35 of the 41 sit in those two districts).

Decision 3 · the sweetener

Guarantee, yes or no?

Yes · a conditional guarantee on the program Recommended

“Run the program on one block as written. If the follow-up DSA hasn’t moved the deficient nutrients into range by the agreed date, the second round of product is on us.”

For Takes the grower’s risk off the table, which is the real reason cold leads don’t buy; tied to compliance and a re-test we control, so the season can’t sink us. Against Amos and Tom have to agree the terms; only offered where we write the program and do the re-test.

No · run without it

The offer and the route still work; they convert less. A softer line for the call stays available either way: “if the read-back wasn’t worth 15 minutes, the next test is free anyway.”
