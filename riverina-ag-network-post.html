James White brought a Certificate of Appreciation into the office this week. It came from the Riverina Ag Network, for Hybrid-Ag’s support of the Harvest Ball and their events this year. Jimmy’s a RAN member himself, so he handed it over to Jamie Granger, our Sales Manager.

If you farm in the Riverina there’s a fair chance you’ve already met Jimmy. He’s out in paddocks most of the week, and a good number of the growers we’ve started working with this season got to us through him first.

RAN brings young people working in agriculture across the region together, at the Harvest Ball, at panel nights like the one in the video, and at the smaller catch-ups in between. Those are the connections that end up mattering when the season gets busy, which is why we back it.

Thanks to the RAN committee. Photos from the ball and a clip from the panel below. Jimmy’s on our stand at Henty next week if you’d like to catch him in person.
